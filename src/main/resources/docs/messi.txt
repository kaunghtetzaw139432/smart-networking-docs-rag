Lionel Messi (The GOAT) Biography:
- Full Name: Lionel Andrés Messi
- Birth Date: June 24, 1987
- Birthplace: Rosario, Argentina
- Current Club: Inter Miami (MLS)

Career Journey:
- FC Barcelona (2004–2021): Won 34 trophies, 10 La Liga titles, and 4 Champions Leagues.
- Paris Saint-Germain (2021–2023): Won 2 Ligue 1 titles.
- Inter Miami (2023–Present): Won the Leagues Cup.

International Career:
- FIFA World Cup 2022: Champion (Golden Ball winner).
- Copa América 2021: Champion.
- Finalissima 2022: Champion.
- Argentina's all-time leading goalscorer.

Individual Records:
- 8 Ballon d'Or awards (World Record).
- 6 European Golden Shoes.
- Most goals scored for a single club (Barcelona - 672 goals).

Playing Style:
Famous for low center of gravity, extraordinary dribbling, vision, and playmaking ability. Often considered the greatest football player of all time.
